# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Vite + Tailwind CSS v4 (`@tailwindcss/vite`), static build. Hosted on GitHub Pages under the user's domain at `almirdev.com/nep` (repo `jose-almir/nep`, Vite `base: '/nep/'`). Local only until the user asks to publish.

## Users

Visitors at an event in Fortaleza (presentation on 2026-12-10) and the people behind the NEP project showing it there. Many will open it on a phone; it may also be shown on a laptop or projector. Exact audience (students, community, beekeepers, researchers) not confirmed.

## Product Purpose

Present plant species from the Ubajara (CE) region that feed bees, when each one flowers, and maps of the region marking where those species occur. Success: a visitor can find a species, see its flowering period, and see where it grows in Ubajara.

## Positioning

A local survey: species and maps catalogued by the NEP team for Ubajara specifically, not a generic bee-plant list.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Species list with popular name, scientific name, flowering months, short description.
- Maps of Ubajara with the species marked. Format unknown (image, KML or coordinates); this decides static vs interactive map.
- Search/filter of species.
- Deadline: 2026-12-10.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: NEP, "Natureza em Equilíbrio pela Polinização". Slogan: "Mais flores, mais abelhas, um futuro melhor."
- Visual identity from the approved concept image: bee-in-hexagon logo, yellow and black, honeycomb motif. The user confirmed this identity is binding.
- Language: Brazilian Portuguese.

## Evidence on Hand

- Concept mockup only. Species shown in it (Ipê-amarelo, Limão, Girassol, Maracujá) are concept examples, not the confirmed catalog.
- Photos and logo in `src/img/` are low-resolution crops of the mockup; to be replaced with originals.
- Missing (requested from the team): real species catalog, maps, logo file, texts about the project, contacts and social links. Do not invent these.

## Product Principles

- Flowering time is the core fact; it must be visible at a glance for every species.
- Placeholders are honest: anything not yet delivered is shown as a marked empty box, never as invented content.
- Phone first: the page must work fully on a phone at the event.
