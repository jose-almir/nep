Scope: single page `index.html`. Mode: Read (visitor understands which plants feed bees, when they flower, where they grow), opening with a Persuade-style hero from the concept.
Audience/job: event visitors in Fortaleza, mostly on phones; find a species, see its flowering months, see the Ubajara map.
Constraints: concept mockup layout and identity are pinned by the user. Undelivered content (maps, real catalog, contacts, socials) shows as X-marked placeholder boxes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The concept page, made phone-first and honest. The flowering calendar is the one thing it owns; it refuses a generic species card grid where blooming time is a footnote.
OWN-WORLD: Black, honey yellow (#F7C51E) and white; honeycomb hexagon line pattern in yellow; pill-shaped labels and buttons; rounded heavy sans (Nunito 800–900) with Caveat for the single handwritten note; nature photography.
STORY: Hero says what NEP is → species with their months → maps of Ubajara → why bees matter / how to help → footer.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Logo + NEP wordmark, the hero headline "Conectando abelhas e flores", bee photo, one CTA to the species.
SIGNATURE: Every species card carries a 12-month strip; month chips filter "o que floresce em <mês>", current month marked.
RISK: Low-res photos cropped from the mockup until originals arrive.
